# HR Analysis Report

## 📊 Report Objective
The objective of this report is to provide a clear and interactive view of workforce status, employee performance and retention risks. 
It helps HR teams monitor key trends, identify areas requiring attention, and support data-driven decisions related to workforce planning, rewards, development and employee retention.

## 📝 Report Contents

## Overview
- Provides a high-level view of the workforce, including total employees, new hires, employee exits, average tenure, salary, satisfaction, and overtime.
- It also highlights monthly headcount and turnover trends, workforce distribution by department and education level, salary ranges, and recently exited employees.

## Performance
- Focuses on employee performance and retention indicators. 
- It summarizes promotion rate, salary raise rate, monthly bonus, and training hours, while providing detailed employee-level performance data.
- The page also identifies employees with potential satisfaction and flight risks by performance rating and department.

---

## 🎯 Benefits
- Provides a centralized view of key workforce and performance metrics.
- Helps HR teams identify turnover, satisfaction, and flight-risk trends early.
- Supports workforce planning through insights into headcount, tenure, salary, promotion, and training.
- Enables faster, data-driven decisions with interactive filters and employee-level details.

---

## ⚙️ How to Use

1. Open the `.pbix` file in the `report/` folder.  
2. Connect to the dataset (update if new data is available) in the `dataset/` folder.  
3. Explore the interactive dashboards to monitor pending and resolved claims.
